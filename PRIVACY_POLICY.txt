1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

SkyWeb ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
================================================================================

2.1 INFORMATION PROVIDED DIRECTLY BY YOU

We collect information you provide to us directly, including:

• Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and 
  mailing address
• Communication Data: Messages, inquiries, and feedback sent through contact 
  forms, email, WhatsApp, or other communication channels
• Project Information: Details about your business, requirements, and 
  preferences relevant to our services
• Payment Information: Billing details, transaction history, and payment 
  methods (processed securely through third-party providers)
• Account Information: Passwords, usernames, and profile data if you create 
  an account with us

2.2 AUTOMATICALLY COLLECTED INFORMATION

We automatically collect certain information when you visit our website:

• Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies to enhance your browsing 
  experience and gather analytics data
• Device Information: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, 
  and referral source
• Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, clicks, scroll depth, and 
  interaction patterns
• Location Data: General geographic location based on IP address (not precise 
  location unless you provide it)
• Analytics: We use tools like Google Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to 
  understand user behavior

2.3 THIRD-PARTY INFORMATION

We may receive information about you from third parties, including business 
partners, service providers, and publicly available sources, to verify 
information and enhance our services.

================================================================================

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

================================================================================

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

• Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about

• Right to Rectification: You can request that we correct inaccurate

• Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your data (subject to legal
